### What is Artificial Intelligence?
 
AI is a science of making machines perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
 
---
 
### History of AI
 
**1950 — Can Machines Think?**
 
Alan Turing designed a test to answer this question, called **"The Turing Test."**
 
**How the Turing Test works:**
- A Human sits in Room A, a Machine sits in Room B.
- A Human Judge asks the same question to both, without knowing which is which.
- If the Judge cannot identify which answer came from the Human and which from the Machine, the Machine **passes** the Turing Test.
**1955 — The term "Artificial Intelligence" was coined**
 
The word was first introduced by **John McCarthy**, often called the "Father of Artificial Intelligence." Researchers came together with an ambitious belief that machine intelligence was achievable.
 
**1986 — "Synthetic Intelligence"**
 
Scientist **John Haugeland** proposed the term "Synthetic Intelligence," sparking a debate — is machine intelligence *real* intelligence (Synthetic) or just an imitation (Artificial)?
 
**1997 — Deep Blue vs Garry Kasparov**
 
IBM's supercomputer **Deep Blue** defeated world chess champion **Garry Kasparov**. This was the first time the question arose: *"Have machines become smarter than humans?"*

### Evolution of AI — The Timeline
 
#### 1. Rule-Based AI (1950–1980)
 
- Simply a collection of rules (if/else statements).
- Example: Checking if a patient has flu —
```
  if (fever && cold && body ache) {
      return flu
  }
```
- Limitation: Rules had to be manually written for every situation. No real learning involved.
#### 2. Machine Learning (1990s)
 
- Machines learn through examples instead of fixed rules.
- Example: Distinguishing Cat vs Dog by training on 1 million labelled pictures.
- First time a machine could:
  - Learn patterns from data
  - Make predictions
- Trade-off: Required a lot of data for training.
- **Shift: Rules ❌ → Examples ✅**
#### 3. Deep Learning / Neural Networks (2000s)
 
- First time a computer could learn features **by itself** — without a human explicitly telling it what to look for (e.g., discovering what an "eye" is, on its own).
- Using Neural Networks, breakthroughs emerged in:
  - Image Recognition
  - Speech Recognition
  - Translation
- Enabled by the GPU revolution + Internet + availability of large datasets.
#### 4. Computer Vision Revolution (2012)
 
- The dataset **ImageNet** was introduced — billions of images, categorized into 1,000+ categories.
- Large neural networks were trained on this dataset.
- In 2012, **Alex Krizhevsky** and team created a deep neural network called **AlexNet**, which massively outperformed previous methods.
- This led to real-world features like:
  - Face Unlock
  - Self-Driving Cars
  - X-Ray analysis
  - Shopping apps identifying products from photos
- First time a machine could reliably "see" and distinguish between a tree, dog, cat, etc.
#### 5. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
 
- Earlier, it was very hard for machines to understand the *meaning* of a sentence based on context.
- Classic ambiguous example: **"I saw a man with a telescope."**
  - Meaning 1: I used a telescope and saw a man.
  - Meaning 2: I saw a man who had a telescope in his hand.
- This ambiguity problem was solved using Deep Learning + NLP techniques.
#### 6. Transformers (2017)
 
- Considered one of the most remarkable algorithms/systems ever created (introduced in the paper *"Attention Is All You Need"*).
- Classic example: **"The Lion did not cross the river because it cannot swim."**
  - First time a machine could correctly figure out that "it" refers to "the Lion" — understanding context and reference within a sentence.
#### 7. Large Language Models (LLMs)
 
- An LLM is essentially a Transformer trained on a massive amount of data.
#### 8. Generative AI
 
- Before 2017, AI could mainly do: Classification, Predictions, Recommendation.
- Today, AI can *generate* something completely new — e.g., write a poem that has never existed anywhere on the internet before.
#### 9. The ChatGPT Moment (November 2022)
 
- Considered one of the biggest revolutions in the history of mankind.
- Released publicly by **OpenAI** in November 2022.
- Introduced the concept of **RLHF** (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) — helped align model responses with what humans found helpful and appropriate.
- Memory capabilities improved over time.
- Sparked and led the entire modern AI movement.
#### 10. The AI Today
 
- Earlier, AI could only answer questions.
- Today, AI can:
  - Think, Plan, Call APIs, Write Code, Understand
  - Make decisions, Complete tasks, Solve problems, Use tools, Search the web
  - ...and many more capabilities
---
 
### Key Takeaway
 
AI didn't happen overnight — it evolved stage by stage, from rigid rule-based systems to machines that can now learn, see, understand language, and generate entirely new content. Each breakthrough solved a limitation of the one before it.
